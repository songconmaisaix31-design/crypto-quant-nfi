# Market Intelligence Research Policy

- External information creates factors and hypotheses, never real orders.
- Ordinary sentiment changes shadow sizing only; it cannot hard block.
- Critical event, data, or liquidity failures may propose a shadow-only block.
- Provider history is ineligible unless event and available times prove it was
  observable at the simulated decision time.
- Every factor requires purged walk-forward, embargo, provider/factor ablation,
  slippage and latency stress, outage/stale simulations, and multiple-testing
  warnings before shadow promotion.
- A candidate requires non-negative net filter value, no worse drawdown,
  slippage-adjusted profit at least equal to baseline, adequate confidence and
  uptime, and non-HIGH overfit risk.
- Gatekeeper remains authoritative. No score can grant live permission.
